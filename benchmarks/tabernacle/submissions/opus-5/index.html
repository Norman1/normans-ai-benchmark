    The Tabernacle

    Everything here is built to the measurements given in Exodus 25–27, 30 and 36–40. Where scripture gives a number, that number is what you are walking around. Where it is silent — the thickness of the boards, the size of the laver, the height of the lampstand, the shape of the cherubim, the form of the roof — the panel for that object says so.
